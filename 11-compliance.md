If licensed under California Community Care Licensing (Title 22) or operating under a school district / ASES / 21st CCLC agreement, confirm your specific requirements — they typically include:

- Staff background checks (Live Scan / TB clearance) on file before a staff member is left alone with children.
- Up-to-date emergency contact and medical authorization forms for every enrolled child.
- Incident reports retained per your licensing agency's retention period.
- Posted licensing/permit documentation visible at the site, if applicable.
