**Earthquake (Southern California specific):** Drop, Cover, Hold On immediately. Do not evacuate during shaking. After shaking stops, Site Lead assesses the building and leads evacuation to the designated outdoor assembly point if needed. Take the attendance roster and emergency contact binder on every evacuation.

**Fire:** Evacuate immediately via the nearest posted exit to the designated assembly point. Take roster. Do not re-enter the building. Site Lead calls 911 and takes a head count at the assembly point before anything else.

**Lockdown:** Follow the host school's lockdown protocol if co-located on a school campus — lights off, door locked, children silent and out of sightlines from windows/doors, until an all-clear from school administration or law enforcement.

**Lost or missing child:** Freeze the group, do an immediate headcount, assign one staff member to search the last-known area while others hold the group. Notify Site Lead within 2 minutes. If not located within 10 minutes, call the school office and 911.

> Post a laminated one-page version of this section at every site entrance and in every staff binder — do not rely on staff opening this website during an actual emergency.
