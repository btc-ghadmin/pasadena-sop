1. **Redirect.** Verbal redirection, restate the expectation, offer a choice.
2. **Reset.** Brief supervised break from the activity, same area, calm-down conversation with staff.
3. **Parent notification.** For repeated or more serious incidents, staff documents and informs the parent at pickup or by call/text same day.
4. **Site Lead involvement.** Safety incidents (hitting, running off, bullying) always involve the Site Lead and a written incident report, regardless of step above.

We never use physical discipline, food withholding, or public shaming as consequences.
