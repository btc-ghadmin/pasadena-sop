- Every child's file must include current emergency contacts, allergy/medical alerts, and any physician-authorized medication plan before their first day.
- Medication (including emergency medication like an EpiPen or inhaler) is stored per program policy and administered only by a designated, trained staff member — never a general Group Leader without training.
- Minor injuries: clean, treat with first aid, log on an incident form, and note it on the sign-out sheet for the parent to see at pickup.
- Anything beyond minor (head injury, allergic reaction, injury requiring more than basic first aid): call 911 first, then the parent, then the Site Lead files a full incident report within 24 hours.
