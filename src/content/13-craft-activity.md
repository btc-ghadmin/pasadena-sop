A teacher-led activity is a craft or project designed and set up by a teacher. It is intended to be **low-supervision**, allowing children to engage independently while still having teacher support available when needed.

Activities can be inspired by:

- Children's interests
- Seasonal themes (e.g., Earth Day, winter holidays)
- Ongoing classroom projects

Because the activity is designed for independent participation, it must be **clearly organized and easy to follow**.

**Each activity should include:**

- A clearly visible sign with simple instructions
- Materials prepared and neatly laid out
- A sample or example of the finished product
- A marker or writing tool for children to label their work
- A designated area where completed artwork can be displayed
