- Daily: sign-out sheet notes (injuries, notable behavior, "great day" flags).
- Weekly: schedule/activity update via [app / email / flyer].
- Same-day, urgent: any injury beyond a minor scrape, any safety incident, any behavior escalation — phone call, not just a note.
- Non-urgent questions from parents get a response within one business day.
