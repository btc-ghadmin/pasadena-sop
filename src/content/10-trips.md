- Signed parent permission slip required before any child leaves the site, no exceptions.
- Maintain in-ratio staffing plus one designated "float" adult for the trip.
- Carry the full roster, emergency contact binder, first aid kit, and a charged phone at all times off-site.
- Head count at departure, at every transition point, and before returning — minimum four counts per trip.
