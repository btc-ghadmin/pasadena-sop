1. **Classroom pickup.** Assigned staff collect the roster and walk to designated classrooms at dismissal. Cross-check every child against the day's enrollment list before leaving the classroom.
2. **Attendance count.** Take a physical head count at the program site immediately on arrival. Log it against the sign-in sheet or app.
3. **Discrepancy protocol.** If a roster child is missing, notify the Site Lead immediately — do not wait until the end of the block. The Site Lead checks with the school office before contacting a parent.
4. **Late arrivals / drop-ins.** Any child not on the daily roster must have a parent-signed authorization on file before joining.
