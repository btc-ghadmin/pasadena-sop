California licensed afterschool/school-age programs generally require a minimum staff-to-child ratio (commonly 1:14 for school-age care under Title 22 — confirm your program's exact licensed ratio, as it varies by program type). Maintain ratio at all times, including transitions and bathroom breaks.

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Site Lead / Director | Overall safety, staff scheduling, parent escalation, incident reports |
| Group Leader | Direct supervision of assigned group, activity delivery, attendance |
| Float / Support Staff | Covers ratio gaps, restroom escorts, first aid backup |

Post the daily staff assignment and ratio count visibly at the check-in table each day.
