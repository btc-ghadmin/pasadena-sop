> **Non-negotiable:** a child is released only to a parent/guardian or an adult listed on that child's Authorized Pickup List, and only after photo ID is checked against that list.

1. **ID check, every time.** Even for familiar faces. No ID, no release — call the parent to confirm or send another authorized adult.
2. **Sign-out log.** Record the time, child's name, and who picked them up.
3. **Unlisted adult.** If someone not on the list arrives, staff calls the parent directly for verbal authorization and documents it. Never release on a verbal claim alone.
4. **No-show at closing time.** Begin phone calls to parent/emergency contacts at close. If unreachable after [30 minutes / per your policy], follow your program's late-pickup and, if needed, local authority notification procedure.
5. **Custody restrictions.** Any court order restricting a parent's pickup rights must be on file with the office; front-line staff should be briefed on any active restriction, not just the office.
