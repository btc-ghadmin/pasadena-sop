**Beyond The Classroom** — *inviting children to learn beyond the boundaries of the classroom.*

Beyond The Classroom provides a safe, structured, and enriching environment for South Pasadena students after the school day ends. Our purpose is threefold: keep every child safe, give working families a dependable option, and use the time well — homework support, physical activity, and enrichment, not just supervision.

Every procedure in this document exists for one of three reasons: **safety**, **consistency** (so any staff member can step into any role and the day still runs smoothly), and **compliance** (state licensing, school district agreements, and insurance requirements).
